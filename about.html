About Mastering Rumble

The independent fan-built resource for everything Rumble — masteringrumble.com.

Our mission

The main goal of Mastering Rumble is to be the main resource for everything Rumble: for Rumble creators and streamers, for Rumble users and viewers — apps, browser extensions, creator tools, communities, official links, and plain-English guides, all in one place.

Mastering Rumble is an independent project. It is not affiliated with RUM Group Inc., the parent company of Rumble, Rumble Wallet, Rumble Studio, and Quake AI.

The brand

Brand name: Mastering Rumble  ·  Domain: masteringrumble.com

The logo: a smooth diagonal gradient that runs from Rumble Green (#84c742) — most prominent in the bottom-left and top-left corners — fading diagonally toward the deep dark navy of Dark Mode (#061726) on the right. In the exact center sits a large solid black circle, and inside it the bold capital letters "MR" in Rumble Green, set in a heavy blocky sans-serif with slightly rounded corners. The black circle cuts the gradient sharply, creating a high-contrast focal point, while the dark blue side gives depth and balance against the bright green.
