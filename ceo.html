Biography summary

Chris Pavlovski is a Canadian technology entrepreneur, best known as the founder and chief executive officer of the video platform Rumble.

Early life

  • Grew up in the Greater Toronto Area and began building websites as a teenager.
  • Attended the University of Toronto Mississauga between 2001 and 2006.
  • Briefly worked as a network administrator at Microsoft before launching his own companies.

Career

  • 2001: Founded Jolted (later Jolt) Media Group in Toronto and served as chief executive.
  • 2009–2012: Director of marketing for Next Giant Leap, a private space exploration company competing for the Google Lunar X Prize; a 2010 regional finalist for the Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year Award.
  • 2011: Co-founded Cosmic Development, an IT and software services firm which grew to over 150 employees.
  • October 2013: Launched Rumble, a Toronto-based video hosting and cloud platform, as an alternative to YouTube with lighter content moderation policies.
  • Under his leadership, Rumble’s monthly visitors rose from 1.6 million in 2021 to 31.9 million in 2022.
  • 2021: Rumble secured new investment from Peter Thiel and J.D. Vance.
  • Late 2021: Announced merger with SPAC CF Acquisition Corp VI; the combined company began trading on Nasdaq (ticker RUM) in 2022 with an implied enterprise value of about $2.1 billion.
  • Partnered with Trump Media & Technology Group for hosting Truth Social on Rumble’s cloud platform.
  • January 2025: Became a billionaire after Rumble’s stock increased nearly 190% in 2024.

Private life

  • Canadian by birth and of Macedonian descent.
  • Serves on the board of Macedonia 2025, a non-profit focused on economic and educational development in North Macedonia.
  • A critic of internet censorship; in 2024 he criticized the arrest of Telegram founder Pavel Durov in France as a “red line.”
