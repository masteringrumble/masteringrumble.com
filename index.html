What is Rumble?

Rumble is a Canadian-American online video platform, web hosting, and cloud services business headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with U.S. headquarters in Longboat Key, Florida. Founded in 2013 by Chris Pavlovski, Rumble launched as an alternative to YouTube with lighter content moderation policies and a creator-first approach.

Creators on Rumble keep 60% of net ad revenue, can monetize from day one, and get paid once they reach a $50 payout threshold via PayPal, bank transfer, or wire. Rumble also offers Rumble Premium ($9.99/month or $99/year) for viewers who want an upgraded experience.
