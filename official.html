Rumble Bug Bounty

Rumble collaborates with security researchers through its Bug Bounty Program to maintain high security standards.

  • How to report: email bugbounty@rumble.com with a full report (description, severity, impact, steps to reproduce, recommendation). Up to 4 reports per month per email; reports become Rumble’s property.
  • Scope: *.rumble.com, *.quake.ai, *.locals.com. Out of scope: social engineering, DoS/DDoS, and automated scanning or brute force without a clear proof-of-concept.
  • Rewards: based on severity, impact, and quality; typical maximum $1,000 USD, with higher rewards possible for exceptionally critical issues. Paid to your Rumble account, then transferable to PayPal.
  • Timelines: initial response within 5 business days; triage within 10 business days after that (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm ET).

Content policies (summary)

Rumble forbids: pornographic, obscene, or adult/sexual content; copyright-infringing material; trademark misuse; illegal content; racism, antisemitism, and hatred; content promoting or inciting violence or unlawful acts (including support for designated terrorist entities or violent groups); child exploitation and disclosure of personal information (doxxing); libel, slander, and privacy violations. In forums and live chat, charity solicitations, pyramid schemes, advertising unrelated to Rumble, and harassment are also prohibited. Automated software (bots, scripts, scrapers) may not interact with Rumble without prior written permission.

The 4 licensing options (summary)

When you upload a video to Rumble you must pick one agency option, and it cannot be changed without Rumble’s express written permission:

OptionNameWhat it means
AVideo ManagementYou appoint Rumble as your exclusive, worldwide agent to distribute, license, and monetize the video everywhere (including YouTube). You receive Creator Earnings. You cannot remove the video from the Rumble Player during the agency term (50 years, auto-renewing).
BVideo Management (Excluding YouTube)Same as A, but Rumble will not publish your content to YouTube — you keep full YouTube control. You receive Creator Earnings.
CRumble OnlyRumble publishes your content through the Rumble Player only. You receive Creator Earnings, and you may remove the video at any time.
DPersonal UseRumble hosts the video on its player/sites only. No monetization and no Creator Earnings. You may remove the video at any time.

AI training license: regardless of the option you choose, submitting content grants Rumble the right to use it to train AI and machine learning models, and to sub-license it to third parties for that purpose.

Creator earnings & agency fees (summary)

Rumble collects earnings on your behalf and pays you Creator Earnings — the revenue after Rumble’s agency fee and third-party costs (processing fees, hosting, taxes, etc.):

Revenue streamRumble agency fee
Programmatic advertising40%
Sponsorship (incl. host-read) advertising40%
Licensing40%
Tipping / Donation20%
Pay-Per-View20%

In plain terms: creators keep 60% of net ad revenue (after fees and costs). Payouts are normally remitted to your Rumble account 30 days after the month Rumble receives payment, once you reach the $50 minimum threshold. No Creator Earnings are paid on Rumble Premium subscriptions.

Reporting copyright infringement — DMCA (summary)

Send a written notice to Rumble’s designated copyright agent:

  • Agent: Claudio R., Rumble Canada Inc., 218 Adelaide Street West, Suite 400, Toronto, Ontario, M5H 1W7
  • Email: dmca@rumble.com

Include your signature, identification of the copyrighted work, the URL(s) of the infringing material, your contact info, a good-faith statement, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you are authorized to act. For many videos or ongoing needs, email dmca@rumble.com with the subject “ONGOING DMCA”. If your material was removed in error, you may send a counter-notification with the same agent details. Knowingly false claims can create liability under Section 512(f).

TAKE IT DOWN Act — nonconsensual intimate imagery (summary)

Under the federal TAKE IT DOWN Act, Rumble must remove validly reported nonconsensual intimate imagery (including indistinguishable deepfakes) within 48 hours and make a good-faith effort to remove copies. To file a report, email moderation@rumble.com with a copy to legal@rumble.com, including: your signature (or your authorized representative’s), links/timestamps locating the content, a good-faith statement that it was published without consent, and contact information.
